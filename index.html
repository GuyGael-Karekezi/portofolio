About me

I am a Mastercard Foundation Scholar pursuing a Master of Science in Engineering Artificial Intelligence at Carnegie Mellon University Africa (expected 2027). Previously, I completed a B.Sc. in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering at the University of Rwanda.

My research interests include healthcare AI, trustworthy machine learning, multilingual NLP, misinformation, financial inclusion, and AI for education. I am particularly interested in learning systems that generalize across settings, datasets, and populations.

Alongside academic research, I work as an AI/ML Developer at ComzAfrica and serve as a Teaching Assistant for Data, Inference and Applied Machine Learning at CMU-Africa.

Afro-FetalNet: Fetal Plane Classification for Low-Resource African Settings

Ishimwe Karekezi Guy Gael, Samuel Olusola, Paul Mugume, Patricia Muriira, Carine Pierrette Mukamakuza

Research on fetal ultrasound plane classification under cross-country domain shift. Developed and evaluated deep learning methods for generalization across African healthcare datasets. The project reports an improvement from 57% to 98.7% F1-score.
